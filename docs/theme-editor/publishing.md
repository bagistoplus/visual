# Preview & Publish

The editor keeps two versions of your store: the **draft** you are working on and the **live** version customers see. This page explains how to check your draft and when to publish it.

## Your work is saved automatically

There is no save button. Every change is saved to the draft as you make it, so you can close the editor and come back later without losing anything. Customers do not see the draft.

## Undo and redo

Use the arrows in the top bar, or the keyboard:

| Action | Windows and Linux | Mac |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Undo | Ctrl + Z | Cmd + Z |
| Redo | Ctrl + Y or Ctrl + Shift + Z | Cmd + Shift + Z |

Undo works on the changes made since you opened the editor. To keep something you might need later, hide it rather than remove it.

## Checking your draft

### In the editor

Use the device menu in the top bar to check the page at different sizes:

<img src="/editor-guide/device-preview.png" alt="The device preview menu" width="292">

- **Auto** fits the preview to the space available.
- **Mobile**, **Tablet** and **Desktop** show typical screen widths.
- **Custom Size** lets you type any width and click **Apply**, for example to check a large phone or a small laptop.

### In a new tab

Click the **eye icon** in the top bar. Your store opens in a new browser tab with your draft, without the editor around it. Click through it as a customer would: open products, change language, add to cart.

## Publishing

When the draft is ready, click **Publish** in the top bar.

<img src="/editor-guide/publish-modal.png" alt="The publish confirmation" width="349">

Click **Publish** in the confirmation to make your changes live. Tick **Don't ask next time** if you do not want the confirmation again on this browser.

::: warning Publish sends everything
Publishing makes the **whole draft** live at once: every page and template, every language and channel, and the theme settings. If you are in the middle of a larger change on another page, finish it or hide it before publishing.
:::

## A checklist before you publish

- [ ] Checked the changed pages on **Mobile**, **Tablet** and **Desktop**.
- [ ] Clicked the main buttons and links in the preview tab.
- [ ] Switched through every language you changed, and translated new sections.
- [ ] Set alt text on new images.
- [ ] Hidden anything that is not ready yet.

## After publishing

The live store now matches your draft. Keep editing as usual: new changes form a new draft that customers will not see until you publish again.

If your store uses a page cache, it may take a moment for every visitor to see the update.
