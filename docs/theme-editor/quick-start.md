# Quick Start

This walkthrough takes about 15 minutes and covers the editor's main moves on the home page: editing text and images, setting your brand color, adding and arranging a section, checking mobile and publishing.

Before you start, open the editor: **Bagisto Visual** > **Themes** > **Customize**. The editor opens on the **Home page**.

## Step 1: Change the hero text

1. In the preview, click the big heading of the hero banner (*Made for everyday, built to last* in the demo store).
2. The settings panel shows **Heading Text**. Type your own headline.
3. Click the smaller text below the heading in the preview and replace it with a short sentence about your store.

The preview updates while you type. There is no save button: your draft is saved automatically.

![Editing the hero heading](/editor-guide/selected-block.jpg)

## Step 2: Change the hero image

1. In the **Page content** tree on the left, click **Hero Banner**.
2. In the settings panel, open the **Appearance** group.
3. Under **Background Image**, click **Change image**.
4. Click **Add images** (or drop a file on the panel), then pick your image and click **Select**.
5. Click **Edit metadata** to describe the image for screen readers, and drag the blue dot onto the most important part of the photo so it stays visible on small screens.

More on images in [Working with Images](./images.md).

## Step 3: Apply your brand color

1. Click the gear icon in the left rail to open **Theme Settings**.
2. Open **Colors**. You see the store's color schemes.
3. Click the **default** scheme to edit it.
4. Change **Primary** to your brand color. Primary is used for main buttons, links and calls to action.

Every section that uses this scheme picks up the new color at once. See [Theme Settings](./theme-settings.md) to learn what each color does.

## Step 4: Add a product section

1. Click the stack icon in the left rail to go back to **Page content**.
2. Under **Main**, click **Add section**.
3. Open the **Product** category and click **Featured Products**.
4. The new section appears at the end of the main area and is selected. In its settings:
   - Leave **Parent Category** empty to show products from your whole catalog, or pick a category.
   - Set **Product Type** to **Featured Products**.
   - Choose how many products to show and whether to display them as a **Grid** or a **Carousel**.
5. Click the heading inside the new section and give it your own title.

::: tip Which products are "featured"?
Featured products are the ones with **Featured** turned on in their Bagisto product settings. The editor only decides how they are displayed.
:::

## Step 5: Move it into place

The new section was added at the bottom. To move it higher:

- Drag its row in the **Page content** tree to the position you want, or
- Select it and click the up arrow in the toolbar above it in the preview, or
- Use **⋮** > **Move to Previous** in the settings panel.

## Step 6: Check mobile

Click the device menu in the top bar (it says **Auto**) and choose **Mobile**. Scroll through the page. If something looks too large or too tight on a phone, most layout settings can have a separate mobile value. See [Settings that change per device](./settings.md#settings-that-change-per-device).

![The home page in mobile preview](/editor-guide/mobile-preview.jpg)

Switch back to **Auto** when you are done.

## Step 7: Preview and publish

1. Click the eye icon in the top bar. Your store opens in a new tab with your changes, so you can click around like a customer.
2. When you are happy, click **Publish** and confirm.

Your changes are now live. Anything you edit from here on is a new draft until you publish again.
