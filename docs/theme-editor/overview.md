# Theme Editor Overview

The theme editor is where you design your storefront. You change text, images, colors and layouts while a live preview of your store updates next to you. You do not need to write code.

## Opening the editor

In the Bagisto admin, go to **Bagisto Visual** > **Themes** and click **Customize** next to your theme. The editor opens on the home page.

## Five ideas to know first

Everything in this guide builds on these five ideas.

**Pages.** Each type of page in your store (home, product, category, cart and so on) has its own design. You pick the page to edit from the selector at the top of the editor. See [Pages & Templates](./pages-templates.md).

**Areas.** Every page is split into three areas: **Header**, **Main** and **Footer**. The header and footer are shared by all pages, so a change there shows up everywhere. The main area belongs to the page you are editing.

**Sections.** A section is a full width band of the page, such as a hero banner, a product list or a newsletter signup. You stack sections to build a page.

**Blocks.** Blocks are the pieces inside a section: a heading, a button, an image, a product price. Blocks can hold other blocks, which is how you build columns and more complex layouts.

**Draft and live.** Your edits are saved automatically as a draft. Customers keep seeing the live version of your store until you click **Publish**. See [Preview & Publish](./publishing.md).

## Page settings and theme settings

There are two kinds of settings, and knowing which one to use saves a lot of time.

| | Page settings | Theme settings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where | The settings panel, after you select a section or block | The gear icon in the left rail |
| Affects | Only the section or block you selected | Your whole store |
| Examples | Hero text, banner image, spacing of one section | Logo, brand colors, fonts, button style |

A good habit is to set up your theme settings first (logo, colors, fonts), then design the pages. See [Theme Settings](./theme-settings.md).
